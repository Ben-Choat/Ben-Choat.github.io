Reservoir Manager

You are the water resource manager for a reservoir serving a growing city, farms, and a hydroelectric facility. Your job: balance water supply, flood safety, and downstream needs over a full 52-week water year.

Each week you receive probabilistic forecasts (95% CI) for both inflow and demand. Decide how much to release — then actuals are revealed and storage updates.

ZoneStorageScoring
Dead Pool0 – 100Water unreachable by gravity. −20 pts/wk
Conservation Pool100 – 600Target zone. +10 pts/wk + up to +12 bonus near target (350)
Flood Control Pool600 – 850Reserved for flood capture. 0 pts/wk
Surcharge Pool850 – 1,000Structural risk zone. −15 pts/wk

Downstream demands — your release must also cover these each week:

DemandSeasonTypical range
Urban water supplyPeaks in summer5 – 15 units/wk
Agriculture (irrigation)Peaks in summer, near zero in winter0 – 25 units/wk
HydroelectricPeaks in winter2 – 14 units/wk

Release below total demand (+ 15 unit env. flow) incurs a shortfall penalty. You may choose to ration during droughts — but it costs points.

Tip: Build up storage in spring so you have carryover for summer demand. The CI on forecasts is wide — plan for the range, not just the mean.
