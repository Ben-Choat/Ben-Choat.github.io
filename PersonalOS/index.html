About

To get this personal assistant app up and running and connected to google mail/calendar I had to have an app page with a privacy policy and terms of service.

Privacy Policy

Personal OS is a locally run tool. If you connect Google Calendar, it reads events from the calendar you select to show your schedule. It saves event times and short titles in your private workspace and stores Google access credentials in your computer’s OS keyring.
Personal OS does not sell calendar data or use it for advertising or model training. If you ask an AI assistant to use your workspace, that assistant may process the information under its own privacy terms. Your data remains on your device until you delete it; you can revoke calendar access through your Google Account.

Terms of Service

Personal OS is a locally run tool provided as-is for personal use. You are responsible for your use of the app and should verify calendar information before relying on it.
